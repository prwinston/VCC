Featured testimony

A Testimony Still Being Written

I accepted Jesus Christ as my Lord and Saviour in 2002. But over time, I have come to understand something more accurate — it was not I who accepted Him. It was He who accepted me.

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.” — Ephesians 2:8–9 (KJV)

If I were to recount every answered prayer, every miracle, every healing, every tender mercy across these years, I would never reach the end of the list. The psalmist said it better than I ever could:

“O LORD my God, you have performed many wonders for us. Your plans for us are too numerous to list. If I tried to recite all your wonderful deeds, I would never come to the end of them.” — Psalm 40:5 (NLT)

What I know with certainty is this: He is still working on me.

“For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.” — Ephesians 2:10 (KJV)

The key word is walk. Not merely know. Not merely believe in theory. Walk — daily, obediently, intimately — with the Lord Jesus Christ, led by the Holy Spirit who guides us into all truth.

Each day, I make a choice to deny myself, take up the cross, and follow Him. I ask Him to examine my heart, to reveal the true condition of my thoughts, and to lead me to genuine repentance where it is needed. And He is faithful — not to condemn, but to cleanse.

“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.” — 1 John 1:9 (KJV)

He knows it all — every deed, every thought, every hidden place in me. And still, He is gracious. Still, He is faithful. Still, He is working on me, inside out and outside in.

This is not a testimony of arrival. It is a testimony of a life still being shaped by a God who has never once let go.

Anita Dharwan
Getting here

Directions & Parking

We meet at 1001, Level 10, Faber Towers, Jln Desa Bahagia, Taman Desa, 58100 Kuala Lumpur. Enter the complex via Jalan Desa Bahagia and go to Tower 1, and request the security guard to grant you access to Level 10.

Open in Google Maps
Option 1 · Recommended
Indoor Parking
Inside Faber Towers

Enter via Jalan Desa Bahagia, follow the ramp into the podium block, and take a ticket at the boom gate. Park at basement levels (B1, B2) or the podium levels near the shops.

  • Covered & shaded, direct lift access
  • Safest for longer visits
  • Busiest at weekday lunch & evenings
Option 2 · Best on Sundays
Outdoor Paid Parking
Open-air lots around Faber Towers
⭐ Sundays: flat RM2 per entry

Lots run along and behind Jalan Desa Bahagia, right outside the building, plus side lots by the shop rows. Mon–Sat use standard hourly rates (pay by machine or app).

  • Cheaper than indoor, plenty of space on weekends
  • Exposed to weather; can fill at office peak hours
Option 3 · If full
Nearby Alternatives
A short walk away
  • Public DBKL bays on Jalan Desa Bakti / Jalan Desa Utama — 3–5 min walk
  • dé Market podium parking — same complex, extra spaces on upper levels
  • Please avoid residential visitor parking at nearby condos (Faber Heights, Danau Desa) — risk of clamping or towing
